# viva la vida – signs and meanings (19)

Custom gestures, not asserted to be standard ISL. The sentence gets the **Word**; the **Meaning** is shown in Practice and the Dictionary.

| # | Sign | Name | Meaning | Word added to sentence | How to make it |
|---|------|------|---------|------------------------|----------------|
| 1 | ✊ | Fist | Hello / Goodbye | Hello | Closed fist, hand upright (fingers pointing up or down). |
| 2 | 🤛 | Left-facing fist | Fist bump / Cheers | Cheers | Closed fist with the hand turned sideways so the knuckles face the LEFT of the screen. |
| 3 | 👍 | Thumbs up | Approval / Good job / Agreement | Good job | Fist with the thumb straight up. |
| 4 | 👎 | Thumbs down | Disapproval / Bad / Disagreement | Bad | Fist with the thumb straight down. |
| 5 | ☝️ | Index up | Number 1 / Attention / Look up | One | Index finger pointing up, other fingers folded. |
| 6 | 👇 | Index down | Look down / Pointing down | Look down | Index finger pointing down, other fingers folded. |
| 7 | 👈 | Point left | Left / Pointing left | Left | Index finger pointing to the left of the screen, other fingers folded. |
| 8 | 👉 | Point right | Right / Pointing right | Right | Index finger pointing to the right of the screen, other fingers folded. |
| 9 | ✌️ | Two fingers (V) | Peace / Victory / ASL letter 'V' | Peace | Index and middle fingers up in a clear V; ring and pinky folded. |
| 10 | 🤞 | Crossed fingers | Hope / Good luck / ASL letter 'R' | Good luck | Index and middle fingers up and crossed (or touching); ring and pinky folded. |
| 11 | 🤟 | I love you | I love you | I love you | Thumb, index and pinky out; middle and ring folded. |
| 12 | 🤘 | Rock sign | Rock on / Horns / Metal salute | Rock on | Index and pinky up; middle and ring folded; thumb folded. |
| 13 | 🤙 | Call me | Call me / Shaka / Hang loose / ASL letter 'Y' | Call me | Thumb and pinky out; the other three fingers folded. |
| 14 | 3️⃣ | Three fingers | Number 3 | Three | Index, middle and ring up; pinky and thumb folded. |
| 15 | 4️⃣ | Four fingers | Number 4 | Four | Index, middle, ring and pinky up; thumb folded across the palm. |
| 16 | ✋ | Raised hand | Stop / Wait | Stop | Open upright hand, all five fingers extended. |
| 17 | 🖖 | Vulcan salute | Live long and prosper | Live long and prosper | Four fingers up and split in two pairs: index+middle together, ring+pinky together, a clear V between middle and ring. |
| 18 | 🫲 | Leftwards hand | Please / Give me / Offer | Please | Open hand held sideways with the fingers pointing to the left of the screen. |
| 19 | 🙌 | Raising hands (2 hands) | High-five / Celebrate / Hooray | Celebrate | BOTH hands open and raised, fingers up, held clearly apart from each other. |

To change a meaning: edit `translator/static/translator/js/signs.js` (fields `m` = meaning, `w` = word). No retraining is needed.
